# Florida City Dashboard — Start Here

Map-first tool to compare Florida cities and counties fairly, show the full property-tax bill over time, and explain what Amendment 3 means for services people use.

## Scope
- Florida only. 67 counties, 411 municipalities.
- City and county peer comparison. State as context only.
- Property tax stack over time: city + county + school + special districts.
- Amendment 3: passed Nov 3, 2026, certified Nov 17, 2026 (61.98% yes). Exemption rises to $150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028, non-school. Non-homestead cap 10% to 5% from Jan 1, 2027.
- 5 years spending, 10 years millage. No house-by-house map.
- Special districts: out of v1 waste ranking, in the tax stack.

## Folders
- /src — map, filters, charts (to be built)
- /etl — scripts that pull and clean AFR / millage data
- /data — cleaned public CSVs only
- /docs — method, sources, review brief
